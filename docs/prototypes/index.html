Five ways to tell the Chatter story

Each prototype explains the same thing: what Chatter is for, what each package does, and how to start. Each one uses motion in its own way. They are built for phones first, so scroll slowly and tap the controls.

  1. G

    Switchboard

    Chatter as an old telephone exchange. Messages are calls patched through brass jacks, party lines and trunk lines.

    Open prototype
  2. H

    Signal

    A cinematic scroll journey. A single point of light becomes a Command, an Event and a Query, then crosses between services.

    Open prototype
  3. I

    Kinetic

    Poster typography in motion. The words themselves are the messages, and they split, stack and re-route as you scroll.

    Open prototype
  4. J · Published as the site

    Message City

    An isometric toy city. Services are buildings and handlers are rooms. Parcels travel the roads between them.

    Open prototype
  5. K

    Playground

    The page is a live two-service simulation. Each chapter unlocks a new control: send Commands, switch transports, break the broker.

    Open prototype
